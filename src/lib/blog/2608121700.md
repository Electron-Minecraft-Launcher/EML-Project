It has now been **one year** since EML Lib and EML AdminTool launched with their initial beta versions. Over the past year, we’ve added numerous features to both tools: the EML Template boilerplate, Fabric support, profiles, skin management, crash statistics and reports, and, most recently, custom loaders.

As EML prepares to move to v3 and eventually v4, we’d like to hear your thoughts: how do you use EML? Is it effective? What do you think of the latest announcement?

Please take a moment to fill out this **short survey**; it’s the best way to support our work!
